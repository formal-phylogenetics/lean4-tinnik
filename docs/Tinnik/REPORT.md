# Audit of TINNiK and its formalization

Paper: E. S. Allman, H. Baños, J. D. Mitchell, J. A. Rhodes, *TINNiK: inference of the tree of blobs of a species
network under the coalescent model*, [Algorithms Mol. Biol. 19 (2024), 23](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13015-024-00266-2).
No author TeX source exists for it. The audit was made against the publisher's XML (PMC11539473), which includes TeX for
the equations but is not the authors' original TeX source. "Line N", or "TINNiK's text, line N", is a line of the plain
text of that XML, one line per paragraph, as lean4-phylo-tree-of-blobs's [`docs/CONFIGURATION.md`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylo-tree-of-blobs/blob/05a024a837076455c700e5d0f88637aa22432b45/docs/CONFIGURATION.md) defines it. Section
and result numbers are the paper's.

This paper is one of the four of the hub [lean4-phylo-tree-of-blobs](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylo-tree-of-blobs), a sub-hub of
[lean4-phylo-network](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylo-network). The findings are numbered
across the collection, and this report keeps those numbers: E29–E33, E40 and E75 on the paper, E82 on Mitchell et al. 2019, a
source it uses, and E34 for the typos.

## 1. Summary

- **Errors.** TINNiK has a misprint in the count of quartets sharing three taxa (E29), and its cut model is an open set
  on which the MLE need not exist at some counts with a zero (E31); neither affects a result. The typos are in E34.
- **Gaps.** E30, E32, E33, E40. All are repaired, and none changes a statement. E75 says what line 202's
  "straightforward" generalization to missing taxa needs: each set of four taxa on more and more gene trees. Nothing
  there is an error.
- **Missing hypotheses.** None found (Section 4).
- **Redundant hypotheses.** Section 5: `0 ≤ δ` in Theorem 4; the star test, which the consistency claim does not need,
  though the proof at step 2a uses it; and for the output of the T3 option, no anomalous quartets on the subsets that
  the tree of blobs does not resolve. Of a cited result: `φ ∈ (0, 1]` in Mitchell et al.'s Proposition 4.2.
- **Use of cited results.** TINNiK's Theorem 2 is applied beyond its printed hypothesis (b) (E30); the stronger rule it
  needs holds by the same proof. Its Theorem 1, Rhodes 2020's Theorem 2 and Mitchell et al.'s Theorem 3.1 are used
  correctly; Theorem 1 also excludes the centroid, so the separate analyticity argument is unnecessary (Section 11).
  Among results only cited, Studier and Keppler's Lemma fails at polytomies, a failure of wording if the letter means
  binary trees, and their theorem holds (Section 2). Mitchell et al.'s Appendix C has a misprint in one display, and its
  Proposition 5.2 holds as printed (E82).

### Gaps in the proofs of cited results

Proving the cited results by their sources' arguments exposed one gap. Studier and Keppler's theorem holds, and their
Lemma fails at polytomies, if it isn't meant for binary trees only. Section 3 has the later point on a cited work (E82)
and the typos of the sources (E34).

- **Studier and Keppler 1988, neighbor joining (a failure of wording, if the letter means binary trees).** Assuming "a tree in which all branches are positive", page 730 attributes to Saitou and Nei the Lemma "If i and j are neighbors, then S_ij is the strictly least element in its row and column", with the proof "Each summand in formula (5a) is positive". It fails at a polytomy: on a 4-star with unit weights all `S_ij` are equal ([`External.StudierKeppler1988.not_lemmaAsPrinted`](../../Tinnik/External/StudierKeppler1988/Lemma.lean#L96)). The letter never defines "neighbors", read here as a cherry of a resolution, and its Figure 1 is binary. If it means binary trees, the star is outside its scope. Their theorem holds, with polytomies too: [`Phylogenetics.NJ.exists_QC_lt_of_not_nbr`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/3fcabfd7d191990dda268b0b3968d945b65eb59a/Phylogenetics/Tree/NeighborJoining.lean#L785), by a different comparison (Section 7).

## 3. Errors and gaps in the paper

**E29. B-inference, Step 3(a)(i).** The number of quartets sharing exactly three taxa with a fixed one is `4(N-4)`, not `4(N-1)`. The order is unchanged. Lean: [`Tinnik.card_sharing_three`](../../Tinnik/Consistency/Theorem4.lean#L38).

**E30. Theorem 2 of the source, as applied.** Hypothesis (b) says that a `t` is a B-quartet "on the induced 4-taxon network", but the Inference Rule also has to fire from a `t` inferred as a B-quartet of the full network, which may be treelike when induced.
This needs a stronger rule than the printed one. It holds by the same proof, because the separating cut edge also resolves `t` on `N`. Lean: [`Allman2023.rule_core`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-allman2023/blob/46efa61d0988df12649089689ae77bc100be034f/Allman2023/LargeNetworks/Rule.lean#L596), from which [`Allman2023.theorem2`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-allman2023/blob/46efa61d0988df12649089689ae77bc100be034f/Allman2023/LargeNetworks/Theorem2.lean#L36) and [`Allman2023.closure_sound`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-allman2023/blob/46efa61d0988df12649089689ae77bc100be034f/Allman2023/LargeNetworks/Rule.lean#L714) follow.

**E31. Appendix C.1.** A maximum need not exist on the open cut model: at the counts `(1,0,0)`, and at any `(n,0,0)` with `n > 0`, the supremum `1` isn't attained ([`Tinnik.not_exists_generatingCutModel_maximizer`](../../Tinnik/AppendixC/OpenCutModel.lean#L108)). Not every count with a zero fails: at `(4,1,0)` an interior maximizer wins, by a computation by hand, noted in the docstring and not in Lean. Lean estimates on the closed simplex, with strictly positive generating parameters, as agreed with the user. [`Tinnik.cutLineMLE_maximizes`](../../Tinnik/AppendixC/LikelihoodRatio.lean#L73).

**E32. Propositions 5 and 6, proofs.** "Assuming `θ₀` is not too close to the boundary … dependent on the sample size" is informal and not needed for fixed `φ₀`. The result holds as stated. In Proposition 6, differentiating under the integral has no justification given: it holds for `x > 0`, by domination on a neighbourhood of `x`, and fails at `x = 0`, where the derivative of the integrand vanishes and `G₁` has a positive right derivative. This does no harm, since `{0}` is null. The density `g = 2 Σ d/dx Gᵢ` also needs `Gᵢ(x) → 0` as `x → 0` ([`ProbabilityTheory.measure_polarStrip_eq_lintegral_of_hasDerivAt`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylo-network/blob/4ed2bb0f7085acbf1326f43e2a5c582ba4566125/PhyloNetwork/ForMathlib/Probability/GaussianPlane.lean#L738)). Every display of the proposition checks out.

**E33. Theorem 4, proof.** Under the alternative, "the test distribution converges to the `χ²₁`" gives no comparison at cutoffs `m d/2` growing with `m`, and "without loss of generality" the MLE on the vertical line hides that the fitted line is random and `d` depends on it. Repairs: the test distribution is pointwise at most that of `Z²`, and the least limit over the three lines gives one `d` ([`Phylogenetics.QuartetTests.cutTest_alternative`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylo-network/blob/4ed2bb0f7085acbf1326f43e2a5c582ba4566125/PhyloNetwork/Testing/Tails.lean#L684), [`Phylogenetics.QuartetTests.cutTail_le_chiSqOneTail`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylo-network/blob/4ed2bb0f7085acbf1326f43e2a5c582ba4566125/PhyloNetwork/Testing/Tails.lean#L148)). Under the null, the paper's argument works and Lean follows it, with two corrections to "`μ₀` goes to `∞` and the distribution converges to the `χ²₁`". First, it should be `|μ₀| → ∞`, as Appendix C.3 writes it, since anomalous strictly cut CFs have `μ₀ → -∞`; the two signs behave alike, since `z̄ ↦ -z̄` swaps the last two terms of `Λ_n`. Second, the limit is governed by `cos α₀ |μ₀|`, the distance from `(0, μ₀)` to the other two lines, and `|μ₀|` alone is not enough: `α₀ → π/2` as `φ₀ → 3/2`, and at fixed `n` there `|μ₀| → ∞` while `cos α₀ μ₀ → -√n`, so the law tends to that of `min(Z², (Z-√n)², (Z+√n)²)`. This does no harm at a positive CF, where `φ̂ → φ₀ < 3/2`. Lean proves the limit uniformly in the argument and the parameter ([`Phylogenetics.Trinomial.abs_cutTail_sub_chiSqOneTail_lt`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylo-network/blob/4ed2bb0f7085acbf1326f43e2a5c582ba4566125/PhyloNetwork/Testing/Trinomial/Tests.lean#L168)), which is what the plug-in needs ([`Phylogenetics.Trinomial.eventually_abs_cutTail_estimate_sub_lt`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylo-network/blob/4ed2bb0f7085acbf1326f43e2a5c582ba4566125/PhyloNetwork/Testing/Trinomial/Tests.lean#L286)); so the p-value is asymptotically uniform ([`Phylogenetics.QuartetTests.cutPValue_asymptoticallyUniform`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylo-network/blob/4ed2bb0f7085acbf1326f43e2a5c582ba4566125/PhyloNetwork/Testing/Limits.lean#L423)) and `P(p < α_m) → 0` for any `α_m → 0` ([`Phylogenetics.QuartetTests.cutTest_null`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylo-network/blob/4ed2bb0f7085acbf1326f43e2a5c582ba4566125/PhyloNetwork/Testing/Limits.lean#L452)). With no 4-blob among the induced networks, "the maximum of the `α'_m`" is over an empty family; any sequence tending to `0` serves, and Lean adds `P(χ²₁ > m)` ([`Tinnik.exists_levels`](../../Tinnik/Consistency/Theorem4.lean#L570)). The centroid step evaluates at hybridization parameters `0` or `1`, on the boundary of the parameter space, so "analyticity" must hold across it: the CFs are analytic on all the coordinates ([`Phylogenetics.RootedNetwork.isGenericNull_of_analyticOnNhd`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/3fcabfd7d191990dda268b0b3968d945b65eb59a/Phylogenetics/NMSC/Generic.lean#L126)). The result holds.

## 10. The paper in the literature

The search was made on 2026-10-31, for TINNiK together with [TOB-QMC](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-tob-qmc/blob/main/README.md) and
[ECToBlob](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-ectoblob/blob/main/README.md), which build on it, in OpenAlex (works citing TINNiK, TOB-QMC, ECToBlob, Allman et al.
2023, Ané et al. 2024, Huber et al. 2025, "Beyond level-1", Rhodes et al. 2025, Xu and Ané 2023, NANUQ+, and the WABI
paper of Dai and Molloy; keyword searches), Crossref (title searches; its `updates` filter for corrections), Europe PMC
and PubMed, the bioRxiv API (versions, journal links), the arXiv API, GitHub's search API, the Archive of Formal Proofs,
and web search. We kept every correction or counterexample found, and otherwise the works that state or claim a result
about the tree of blobs, about which four-taxon information decides it, or about a method that starts from a tree of
blobs with a stated guarantee. Empirical studies, reviews and work on other problems were screened by abstract and left
out. Each statement below comes from the cited work's abstract or numbered statements, and we say where only the
abstract was read. Citation indexes lag, so recent citing work may be missing. A preprint's results are reported as
claimed. This section keeps the works that concern TINNiK; the reports of TOB-QMC and ECToBlob have the others.

### Before the paper

- Solís-Lemus, Ané, [PLoS Genet. 12 (2016)](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pgen.1005896) (SNaQ, pseudolikelihood on quartets); Baños, [Bull. Math. Biol. 81 (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11538-018-0485-4) ([arXiv 2017-11-28](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.10545); read for its Proposition 7): "every cycle of size at least 4 and every hybrid node in a cycle of size at least 5" of a level-1 network are identifiable from gene tree quartets; Allman, Baños, Rhodes, [NANUQ, Algorithms Mol. Biol. 14 (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13015-019-0159-2) ([arXiv 2019-05-16](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.07050)): level-1 inference from quartet hypothesis tests and a quartet intertaxon distance. TINNiK's introduction sets its algorithm against these level-1 methods.
- Allman, Baños, Mitchell, Rhodes, [J. Math. Biol. 86 (2023), 10](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00285-022-01838-9) ([arXiv 2022-05-06](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.03466)): "most features of the unrooted tree of blobs" are determined by the distribution of gene quartet topologies, with no assumption on the level, through a B-quartet inference rule. It names a difficulty that TINNiK meets: a B-quartet of the network can have a cut-edge split on its induced 4-taxon network, "the main obstacle" (compare E30). It is formalized in lean4-allman2023: [its README](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-allman2023/blob/main/README.md).

What the paper adds. TINNiK ([bioRxiv 2024-04-24](https://doi.org/10.1101/2024.04.20.590418)) turns the sketch of Allman et al. 2023 into an algorithm with a consistency theorem (Theorem 4) and writes "We know of no other proposed algorithm for tree of blobs inference from biological data"; ECToBlob and TOB-QMC both describe it as the first. We found no earlier proof of TINNiK's Theorem 4. Section 2 lists the cited results the proofs use, including Mitchell et al. 2019 and Rhodes 2020.

### Concurrent work

- Huber, van Iersel, Jones, Moulton, Veenema-Nipius, [Bull. Math. Biol. 87 (2025), 136](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11538-025-01510-5) ([arXiv 2024-08-23](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.12997)). Theorem 5.1, for binary semi-directed networks, which "does not put any restriction on the level": a nontrivial `A|B` is a cut-edge split exactly when `a₁a₂|b₁b₂` is a cut-edge split of the quarnet for all `a₁, a₂ ∈ A`, `b₁, b₂ ∈ B`. Corollary 5.6: the quarnets determine the blob tree.
- Frohn, Holtgrefe, van Iersel, Jones, Kelk, [J. Comput. Syst. Sci. 152 (2025), 103655](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcss.2025.103655) ([arXiv 2024-09-09](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.06034)). Lemma 1 sharpens Huber et al.'s theorem by fixing `a₁` and `b₁`. Theorem 5 reconstructs the tree of blobs of a binary network of any level in `O(n³)` time from `O(n³)` quarnet-splits, and Proposition 6 gives a lower bound of `Ω(n log n + ℓn)` quarnet-splits at level `ℓ`.

Huber et al. and Frohn et al. work from exact quarnet information, with no statistical model. Both appeared after the TINNiK preprint and do not build on its algorithm.

### Since the paper

We found no erratum, retraction or published counterexample to a statement of TINNiK: Crossref's `updates` filter returned nothing for TINNiK or for Allman et al. 2023, and Europe PMC lists no correction for either. Among the works cited in this section, the only correction we found is that to Holtgrefe et al. 2025, for an omitted figure. The errors recorded in Section 3 are this audit's own.

Versions of the paper:

- TINNiK: no later version found. It is implemented, with ECToBlob and NANUQ+, in MSCquartets 3.3 (Rhodes, Allman, Ané, Baños, Mitchell, Wicke, [Bioinformatics, 2026-10-20](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btag775); [EcoEvoRxiv 2026-06-09](https://doi.org/10.32942/x24d5w); abstract only).

Work building on TINNiK:

- TOB-QMC (Dai, Han, Molloy; first public version [bioRxiv v1, 2025-11-06](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.11.05.686850); published as [Genome Research, doi 10.1101/gr.282203.126](https://doi.org/10.1101/gr.282203.126)) and ECToBlob (Rhodes, Allman, Ané, Baños; [bioRxiv v1, 2026-05-31](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.05.28.728501)), both formalized in their own repositories of this hub ([TOB-QMC](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-tob-qmc/blob/main/README.md), [ECToBlob](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-ectoblob/blob/main/README.md)). TOB-QMC cites the statement of TINNiK's Theorem 4 for the correctness of the tests on all four-taxon subsets, which is a step of its proof, [`Tinnik.tests_correct`](../../Tinnik/Consistency/Theorem4.lean#L494) (E21, in [TOB-QMC's report](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-tob-qmc/blob/main/REPORT.md)).

## 11. What's next

### Open directions

- **Other inheritance.** Theorem 4 is proved under the NMSC with independent inheritance, `ρ = 0`, and the CF-detectability it uses, [`External.Allman2023.cfDetectable_generic`](../../Tinnik/External/Allman2023/CFDetectable.lean#L34), covers only `ρ = 0`. For binary networks at generic parameters, "Beyond level-1" (Proposition 5.1, Section 10) extends the arguments of Allman et al. 2023 to common inheritance, `ρ = 1`, for tree of blobs identifiability, and leaves other `ρ` aside. [`Phylogenetics.geneTreeDist`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/3fcabfd7d191990dda268b0b3968d945b65eb59a/Phylogenetics/NMSC/Defs.lean#L94) includes `ρ`. What is needed first is the generic clause of CF-detectability at that `ρ`: a nonvanishing witness for each 4-taxon network.

### Simpler proofs

Each of these is, or was in an earlier version, the argument of a formal proof, so it is checked in Lean. We found none of them published, in the search of Section 10.

- **TINNiK's Theorem 4: the centroid.** The paper proves `CF ≠ (1/3,1/3,1/3)` for generic parameters by a separate analyticity argument. It follows from CF-detectability: strictly cut and non-cut CFs each have two different entries. This removes that paragraph of the proof, and also the need for a separate generic set. The formal proof of Theorem 4 takes the paper's step; [`Tinnik.tests_correct`](../../Tinnik/Consistency/Theorem4.lean#L494) takes the shortcut, [`Tinnik.noCentroidCF_of_cfDetectable`](../../Tinnik/Consistency/Theorem4.lean#L340).
- **TINNiK Proposition 6.** Computing the strip probabilities in rotated Cartesian coordinates, with Fubini in place of differentiating under the integral, and taking `G₄, G₅` as reflections of `G₃, G₂` at `-μ`, gives the same `d/dx Gᵢ` and needs no domination argument. An earlier version of the formal proof did this (in lean4-phylo-tree-of-blobs's history); the formal proof now follows the paper.
- **The T3 test at the singularity.** Non-collapse needs no limit law: the T3 statistic is at most the T1 statistic, which is tight on the null, and the p-value is at least `½ χ²₁(10 t)`, since `sin² α₀ ≥ 1/10` on `[0,1]`.
- **Wilks-type limits for a trinomial.** The paper uses these limits; for a trinomial there is an elementary proof. One estimate, `|(1+v) log(1+v) - v - v²/2| ≤ 4|v|³` for `|v| ≤ 1/2` ([`Real.abs_entropyExcess_sub_le`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylo-network/blob/4ed2bb0f7085acbf1326f43e2a5c582ba4566125/PhyloNetwork/ForMathlib/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/EntropyExcess.lean#L50)), gives each likelihood ratio statistic as a sum of such terms, so each is uniformly close on bounded sets to a continuous function of the whitened frequencies, and an extended continuous mapping theorem, proved from the portmanteau theorem on open sets (`Phylogenetics.Trinomial.tendstoInDistribution_of_closeOnBalls`), finishes. This does not use van der Vaart's Theorem 16.7. At the centroid, Pearson's statistic is exactly `Z₁² + Z₂²` ([`Phylogenetics.Trinomial.pearsonS_centroid`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylo-network/blob/4ed2bb0f7085acbf1326f43e2a5c582ba4566125/PhyloNetwork/Testing/Trinomial/Whiten.lean#L425)).
- **Neighbor joining.** The side with at most `n/2` clusters is shorter than Studier and Keppler's two-node contradiction, and it covers polytomies.
- **Rhodes Theorem 2.** `{x,y,u,v} ∈ Q` holds exactly when no side contains `u, v` and avoids `x, y`, so count such pairs directly and split them by node, with no case split on the shape of the quartet.
